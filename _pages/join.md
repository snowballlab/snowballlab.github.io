## What we value

The Snowball Lab is always looking for motivated students who are excited about
solving real-world problems through interdisciplinary research. We welcome
applicants from a wide range of backgrounds, including computer science,
electrical engineering, biomedical engineering, design, and related fields.

Experience in any of the following areas is especially valuable:

- Machine learning for activity recognition
- AI for health
- Embedded computing
- Hardware prototyping
